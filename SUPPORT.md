# Support

## Where to ask

- **Questions, setup help and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/1N3/sn1per-mcp/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the [bug report template](https://github.com/1N3/sn1per-mcp/issues/new/choose). Start with [docs/troubleshooting.md](https://github.com/1N3/sn1per-mcp/blob/main/docs/troubleshooting.md) and include `sn1per-mcp --version`, the edition and engine version from `sniper_status`, your client and its version, your OS and your Python version.
- **Edition compatibility reports** ("works on" or "fails on" a Sn1per edition or version): use the edition compatibility issue template.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** never in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/1N3/sn1per-mcp/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **Sn1per Professional and Sn1per Enterprise customers:** e-mail `support@sn1persecurity.com` for help that involves your licence or your installation.

Before you post anything public, remove targets, hostnames, IP addresses, loot content and credentials from logs and screenshots. The bug template asks you to confirm this.

## What to expect

This is a beta. Issues and discussions are triaged weekly. Community support has no fixed response time; paying customers use `support@sn1persecurity.com`.

## Out of scope

- **Bugs in Sn1per itself** (the scan engine, its modes and its output): report them to the [Sn1per project](https://github.com/1N3/Sn1per/issues), or through your customer support channel for Sn1per Professional and Sn1per Enterprise. If you are not sure where a problem belongs, open an issue here and we will route it.
- **Help scanning targets you are not authorized to test** is refused.
